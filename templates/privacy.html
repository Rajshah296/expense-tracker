Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your email address and password. Additionally, we collect the financial data you enter into the app, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes calculating your spending totals, generating category-based insights, and maintaining your user account.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and financial information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics that compromise your privacy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
